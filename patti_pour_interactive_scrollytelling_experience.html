How It Works • Side-of-Pouch Guide

Brew the Perfect Chai.

Tap any step below to inspect the exact temperature, steeping time, and leaf ratio for your selected blend.

1

Boil Water (100°C)

Bring fresh, filtered water to a rolling boil at 100°C (85°C for Kashmiri Kahwa green leaves).

2

Add Tea (1 tsp)

Measure 1 heaped teaspoon (2.5g) of Patti & Pour leaves per 200ml cup into your warmed teapot.

3

Steep (3–5 min)

Cover and let the whole leaves and whole spices bloom for 3 to 5 minutes. Add milk & jaggery if desired.

4

Strain & Enjoy

Pour through a fine mesh strainer into your favorite ceramic cup and savor the story in every sip.
